
Sie hat ein Spiegelartefakt als Amulett, dass versucht sie zu übernehmen. Ihre Magie bewahrt

Das Artefakt dämmt ihre Magie indirekt, da sie konstant magische Energie aufbraucht um sie vor den Effekten des Amuletts zu schützen.

Sie trägt einen hochrangigen Ifriten in sich, der Zerstörung und Chaos will



Sie hatte vor 5 Jahren als sie das Artefakt berührte das erste mal eine Vision



|d20 Roll|Effect Name|Dangerous & Explody Effect|
|--:|---|---|
|**1**|**Supernova**|The spell centers on you and detonates instantly. You and all creatures within a **20-foot radius** must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking **full spell damage** (or 6d6 fire damage, whichever is higher) on a failure, or half on a success.|
|**2–3**|**Molten Backlash**|Superheated plasma coats your spellcasting focus or hands. Your focus is destroyed (if non-magical), and you take **3d6 fire damage**. You drop whatever you are holding and cannot use that hand for somatic components until you receive magical healing.|
|**4–5**|**Ignited Reserves**|The fire ignites your own magical essence. You immediately lose your **highest available spell slot**, and it detonates out of your body, dealing **1d10 fire damage per level of the slot lost** to you and everyone within 5 feet.|
|**6–8**|**Shrapnel Blast**|The fire magic superheats the ground beneath you, causing the earth or stone to explode. You and anyone within 10 feet take **2d10 piercing damage** from flying shrapnel and are knocked prone.|
|**9–11**|**Living Inferno**|You become a walking hazard. You burst into violent, uncontrollable flames for 1 minute. At the start of your turns, you take **2d6 fire damage**, and **any creature or flammable object** that touches you or starts its turn within 5 feet of you takes **2d6 fire damage**.|
|**12–14**|**Chain Reaction**|The spell misses and hits the nearest piece of terrain, structural wall, or unattended object, causing a catastrophic structural collapse. Debris rains down in a **15-foot radius**, trapping caught creatures under heavy rubble (**DC 15 Strength check** to break free).|
|**15–17**|**Blinding Flash**|A blinding magnesium-white flare erupts from your hands. You and all creatures looking at you within 30 feet are **permanently blinded** until cured by a _Lesser Restoration_ spell or higher.|
|**18–19**|**Thermal Vacuum**|The fire suddenly sucks all the oxygen out of the room before exploding outward. All creatures within 15 feet are pulled 10 feet toward you, **suffocating** (cannot breathe or speak) until the end of their next turn, right before a sonic boom deals **2d6 thunder damage**.|
|**20**|**Unstable Overcharge**|The spell doesn't fire, but the energy is trapped. Your hands glow white-hot. At the start of your next turn, you **must** use your action to cast a fire spell, or the energy explodes inside you, dealing **4d6 fire damage** to you automatically.|
