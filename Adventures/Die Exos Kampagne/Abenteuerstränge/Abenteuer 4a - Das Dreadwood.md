Die 3 Hags

We can play a little game. You can choose- something to be given to you, you give something, or have something taken from you. Then, I’ll reveal what it is. And if you don’t like it, you can change to another! If all of you do that, I will give you the child


### The decks


|1d6|A Green Hag Trades Its Knowledge For...|
|---|---|
|1|A bargainer's memories of a loved one.|
|2|The cauldron of a rival hag.|
|3|A favor to be redeemed when the hag wishes.|
|4|A flower from a hidden Feywild garden.|
|5|A gift given freely by a yugoloth.|
|6|A vial filled with a ruler's tears.|

**YOU — the errand** (d10)

1. You give the baron this apple. _(She does not say what it does. It may do nothing at all.)_
2. You give the miller's boy this dagger, in his belly.
3. You give your captain a hug from me. _(Touch delivery. Something rides along.)_
4. You give the abbot this letter, sealed.
5. You give the coven your allegiance — one favour, called at her convenience.
6. You give the dead man an apology. _(You'll need to find out which one, and where he's buried.)_
7. You give my sister back her name. _(It was taken. It is somewhere. Go.)_
8. You give the first child you meet my blessing. _(A kiss on the forehead.)_
9. You give the next beggar you pass everything in your purse, and your boots.
10. You give my name to no one. _(The moment it's spoken aloud, everything here reverses.)_

**GIVE — the gift** (d10)

1. A love of books — you cannot resist reading anything put in front of you. _(Pair with #4 above and watch the table's faces.)_
2. An answer to any one question, delivered when she pleases. Truthfulness not included.
3. A necklace for you to wear. A Hag's Eye. It does not come off.
4. Your mother's voice, perfectly. You will never hear it in memory again.
5. Thick skin. +1 AC; you lose fine touch, disadvantage on anything needing delicate fingers.
6. Your fear, removed. Immunity to _frightened_ — and no instinct that anything is wrong, ever.
7. A friend who will never leave you. _(A crow. It's lovely. It reports back.)_
8. A word that stops one blade, once. The blade remembers you afterwards.
9. One true dream a year, among a great many false ones, unlabelled.
10. A child, someday, with your eyes.

**TAKE — the price** (d10)

1. Your ability to kill. Enemies that should die drop to 1 hp instead.
2. Your last breath. Disadvantage on death saves. _(Softer than two-strikes and still terrifying.)_
3. A fingernail. The whole thing. Now, at the table, in front of everyone.
4. Your reflection. Mirrors show the room behind you. The undead recognise a kinsman.
5. Your shadow — which is hers now, and goes where she sends it.
6. The face of someone you loved. You keep every feeling; you lose the face and the name.
7. Your laughter. The sound of it, specifically. You can still find things funny.
8. Seven years, taken off the back end where you can't check the balance.
9. A pint of blood now, and a pint every new moon, however far away you are.
10. Your apology. You may never again say sorry and be believed.
