
_Large monstrosity, neutral evil_

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 114 (12d10 + 48)  
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 20 ft., fly 40 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|5 (−3)|14 (+2)|8 (−1)|

**Saving Throws** Str +8, Con +7, Wis +5  
**Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +5  
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 15  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 6 (2,300 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +3

---

### Traits

**Keen Smell.** The mothbear has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

**Nocturnal Predator.** While in dim light or darkness, the mothbear has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

**Dark Ambusher.** While hidden in dim light or darkness, the mothbear can fly at half its flying speed without revealing its position. Its position is revealed normally if it attacks, enters bright light, or otherwise does something that would reveal it.

**Light Fixation.** The mothbear always knows the location of any source of bright light within 60 feet of it. If a source of intense light suddenly appears within that range, the mothbear must succeed on a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or be unable to take reactions until the end of its next turn.

---

## Actions

**Multiattack.** The mothbear makes two attacks: one with its **Bite** and one with its **Claws**.

**Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage.

**Claws.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 16 (2d10 + 5) slashing damage.

### Crushing Dive

The mothbear folds its wings and drops like a massive, fur-covered stone onto a creature beneath it.

The mothbear must descend at least **20 feet** immediately before making this attack.

_Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature beneath the mothbear.  
**Hit:** 21 (3d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

The target must succeed on a **DC 16 Strength saving throw** or be knocked **prone**.

If the mothbear was **hidden from the target** when it began the dive, the attack deals an additional **7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage**.

After the attack, the mothbear lands in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the target.

---

### Choking Moth Dust

_Recharge 5–6_

The mothbear violently convulses its enormous wings, releasing a nauseating storm of powdery wing scales, oily fur, and countless microscopic barbed hairs.

Each creature in a **15-foot cone** must make a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**.

On a failed save, a creature takes **21 (6d6) poison damage** and is **blinded until the end of its next turn** as the dust cakes its eyes, throat, and lungs.

On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't blinded.

A creature that doesn't need to breathe has advantage on this saving throw.

The dust hangs in the air until the **start of the mothbear's next turn**, and the affected area is **lightly obscured** for the duration.

---

## Bonus Actions

### Vanish into the Gloom

After the mothbear makes at least one attack on its turn, it can move up to **half its flying speed** without provoking opportunity attacks.

If it ends this movement in **dim light or darkness**, it can immediately take the **Hide action**.

---

## Reactions

### Panic Flutter

When the mothbear is hit by a melee attack, it violently beats its wings at the attacker.

The attacker must succeed on a **DC 15 Strength saving throw** or be pushed up to **10 feet away** from the mothbear.

---

### Typical Hunting Pattern

The mothbear hides high above its prey in darkness, nearly invisible against cavern ceilings or rafters. It then **Crushing Dives** onto an isolated victim, uses **Vanish into the Gloom** to retreat upward, and disappears again.

Once its prey gathers together—or begins looking upward—it unleashes **Choking Moth Dust**, filling the battlefield with glittering, lung-clogging scales before vanishing back into the darkness.

It rarely fights fair.
