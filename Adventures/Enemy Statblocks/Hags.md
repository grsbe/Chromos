




### Gertrude

_Power-obsessed hag, CR 4_

**AC** 15  
**HP** 82  
**Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 18 (+4) **DEX** 12 (+1) **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 14 (+2) **WIS** 14 (+2) **CHA** 16 (+3)

**Saves** Wis +4, Cha +5  
**Skills** Arcana +4, Intimidation +5  
**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.

**Steal Strength.** When a creature within 30 ft. succeeds on an attack roll or saving throw, Gertrude can use her reaction to force it to make a DC 13 Charisma save. On a failure, subtract 1d4 from the roll, potentially turning it into a failure.

Gertrude gains temporary stacks equal to **5 + the number rolled**.

While she has temporary stacks from this feature, her next **Charge** deals extra damage equal to the stacks she currently has, to a maximum of 10. 
She can consume the stacks after a charge to gain that amount of temp hp

#### Actions

**Multiattack.** Gertrude makes two Claw attacks.

**Claw.** +6 to hit, 5 ft.  
Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing + 3 (1d6) force.

**Charge.** Gertrude moves up to her speed in a straight line without provoking opportunity attacks, then makes one Claw attack.

If she moved at least 15 ft. before hitting, the target must succeed on a DC 14 Strength save or be knocked prone.

**Bow to Me — Recharge 5–6.** One creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 13 Wisdom save. On a failure, it takes 13 (3d8) psychic damage and falls prone.

**Spellcasting.** DC 13.  
At will: _detect magic, thaumaturgy_  
2/day each: _command, misty step, ray of enfeeblement_

The combat loop becomes very visible: **someone does something impressive → Gertrude steals it → Gertrude immediately barrels into somebody like an angry magical rhinoceros.**

She should absolutely yell things like:

> “Oh, very impressive. **Mine.**”

and then charge.

### Hildegaart

_Manners-obsessed hag, CR 4_

**AC** 15  
**HP** 78  
**Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 16 (+3) **DEX** 12 (+1) **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 15 (+2) **WIS** 16 (+3) **CHA** 18 (+4)

**Saves** Wis +5, Cha +6  
**Skills** Insight +5, Persuasion +6  
**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.

### The Rules of the House

When the party meets Hildegaart, she **very clearly announces her rules**.

Something like:

> “Shoes wiped. Names given. No interrupting. No shouting indoors. No elbows on the table. And when a lady speaks, you listen.”

This isn't just characterization—it tells the players exactly how she works.

At the start of combat, Hildegaart sternly announces **three rules** appropriate to the scene, such as:

- Do not interrupt.
- Do not raise your voice.
- Address me properly.
- Do not strike someone who is speaking.
- No running indoors.
- Ask permission before casting spells.
- Absolutely no elbows on the table.

**Mind Your Manners.** Once per round, when a creature within 30 ft. knowingly breaks one of Hildegaart's announced rules, she can use her reaction to snap:

**“MANNERS.”**

The offender makes a DC 14 Wisdom save. On a failure, it takes 7 (2d6) psychic damage and has disadvantage until the end of their next turn.

**Good Manners.** A creature that visibly follows one of Hildegaart's rules despite it being inconvenient gains advantage on its next saving throw against her.

#### Actions

**Multiattack.** Hildegaart makes two Cane attacks.

**Cane.** +5 to hit, 5 ft.  
Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning + 3 (1d6) psychic.

**Sit Down.** One creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 14 Wisdom save. On a failure, it falls prone and its speed becomes 0 until the end of its next turn.

If there is an available chair, it is instead magically forced into the chair.

**Apologize — Recharge 5–6.** A creature within 30 ft. that broke one of Hildegaart's rules must make a DC 14 Charisma save. On a failure, it is charmed until the end of its next turn and must use its action to give Hildegaart a sincere apology.

**Spellcasting.** DC 14.  
At will: _prestidigitation, vicious mockery_  
2/day each: _command, hold person, suggestion_

This makes Hildegaart much easier to run because the players **know the joke before the punishment happens**. The fun becomes watching them decide whether they're willing to obey increasingly absurd etiquette during a deadly fight.

And Hildegaart should be almost painfully literal about it. If somebody is bleeding out on her carpet:

> “You are _dripping_.”

If someone casts _fireball_ without permission:

> “Did I hear the word **please**?”

If someone politely asks, “May I stab you?”

> “You may certainly try. Thank you for asking.”

### Portia

_Knowledge- and misery-obsessed hag, CR 5_

Portia does not merely collect secrets. She collects the kind of truths that ruin marriages, friendships, families, and alliances.

**AC** 15  
**HP** 95  
**Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 12 (+1) **DEX** 16 (+3) **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 19 (+4) **WIS** 15 (+2) **CHA** 18 (+4)

**Saves** Int +7, Wis +5, Cha +7  
**Skills** Arcana +7, Deception +7, Insight +5  
**Resistances** psychic  
**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.

**Sow Resentment.** At the start of combat, Portia chooses two creatures she can see that are allies.

Whenever one of them damages an enemy while the other is within 30 ft., Portia can whisper a poisonous thought. The second creature makes a DC 15 Wisdom save. On a failure, it has disadvantage on its next attack roll or ability check unless it targets or acts against the first creature in some way.

Portia changes the pair as a bonus action.

**Secrets Hurt More.** Portia deals an extra 5 (1d10) psychic damage to a creature if she knows one of its meaningful secrets, fears, betrayals, or grudges.

#### Actions

**Multiattack.** Portia makes two Quill-Claw attacks.

**Quill-Claw.** +7 to hit, 5 ft.  
Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing + 4 (1d8) psychic.

**Tell Them What You Really Think — Recharge 5–6.** One creature within 60 ft. makes a DC 15 Charisma save.

On a failure, Portia magically forces a buried resentment to the surface. The creature takes 18 (4d8) psychic damage and must use its reaction, if available, to verbally accuse or insult one ally it can see.

That ally then has disadvantage on its next attack roll, ability check, or saving throw before the end of its next turn.

**Poison the Friendship.** Two creatures within 60 ft. that can hear Portia each make a DC 15 Wisdom save. If both fail, neither can willingly give the other advantage, healing, Help, or beneficial spells until the end of Portia's next turn.

**Spellcasting.** DC 15, +7 to hit.  
At will: _message, minor illusion, vicious mockery_  
3/day: _detect thoughts_  
2/day each: _suggestion, major image_ , misty step
1/day: _enemies abound_
